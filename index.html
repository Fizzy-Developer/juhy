The maths behind every proposal

Worked examples we use when we scope a project. Your own numbers become the baseline we report against.

+33%

Revenue with no extra traffic

10,000 sessions at a 1.5% conversion rate and a $40 order is 150 orders and $6,000. Lift conversion to 2% and it becomes 200 orders and $8,000.

$960

A month, from one cart flow

300 abandoned carts a month at a $40 order. Win back 8% of them with a cart reminder sequence and that’s about $960 every month from existing visitors.

1.67

Break-even return on ad spend

A $50 product that costs $20 all in has a 60% margin, so every $1 of ads must bring back at least $1.67. Below that, each sale from ads loses money.

4.0×

Return on ad spend isn’t profit

$4,000 in sales on $1,000 of ads looks strong. Take off $1,600 product cost, $400 fulfilment and shipping and $120 payment fees and $880 is left, before software, fees and salaries.

Questions we get asked

Why not just use an AI tool instead of an agency?

AI tools are good at first drafts, and we use them too. What they don’t do is decide which part of your funnel to fix first, run and judge ad tests with your money, or take responsibility for the result. That judgment is what you’re paying us for.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. We control the conditions that produce sales: the store, the follow-up, the tracking and the campaigns. Demand, pricing, product quality and stock are outside our control, so promising a sales figure would be dishonest.

What we do promise is a clear scope, a recorded baseline and honest reporting against it. Some clients choose a base fee plus a bonus tied to results we define together.

How long before I see results?

It depends on the work. Cart and welcome flows usually start earning within weeks because they work on traffic you already have. Conversion work runs over about four weeks. SEO takes three to six months to show in search.

My return on ad spend looks good. Isn’t that enough?

Not always. Return on ad spend measures sales, not profit. Once product cost, shipping and fees come off, a 4× return can leave much less than it looks. We work out your break-even point first and report profit, not just sales.

Do you only work with Shopify stores?

Shopify is our core, but we also work with WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and WordPress, plus booking, course and CRM platforms for service businesses like coaches, clinics, schools and real estate firms.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. We match the channel to the market. In Africa, Europe, Latin America, India and the Middle East, WhatsApp sits beside email for cart recovery and updates. In the US, SMS takes that role.

What do you need from me to start?

For the free audit, just your website. We only use what’s public. If you go ahead, we’ll ask for access to your store and analytics, your margins and your current numbers, and we never change anything without your permission.
